## 6. Deliver

Provide model files, test inputs, mapping, provenance, dependencies and report. State bead count, charge, analogues, provisional types, the AA topology used (supplied or engine; its stated engine and charge method), bonded flags, seeds tested, tested timestep/duration, actual checks, deviations and reference exposure. Compute every status, count and list in the report from the run's records (mapping, parameters, analogue search, validation, manifest); never write them from a template. Reports carry no claim about a molecule that its records do not support. List all statements labelled "agent background knowledge, unverified". Report statuses `blocked`, `constructed_unexecuted`, `preprocessed` or `execution_checked`; store physical observables separately. Report the SASA band (pass within 5%, review to 10%, flag above 10%) if the SASA check was run. It changes no model or status, and do not change bond lengths to meet it without a recorded decision. If dependencies prevent completion, list concrete pending actions and deliver available work without claiming success. Do not invent calibrated confidence scores.
